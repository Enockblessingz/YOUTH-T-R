Cookie Policy

How Youth Transformers uses cookies and similar tracking technologies.

1. What Are Cookies

Small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and keep you signed in.

2. Cookies We Use

Essential (always active) — required for login and core features.

  • yt-auth-token — maintains your login session.
  • yt_language — remembers your language preference (EN/RW).
  • supabase-auth — manages authentication tokens with our backend.

Preference (optional) — remember your settings.

  • yt_theme — dark/light mode preference.
  • yt_dashboard_layout — dashboard customization.

3. What We Do NOT Use Cookies For

  • No advertising cookies — we don't serve ads or track you across other sites.
  • No analytics tracking — we don't share usage data with third-party analytics providers.
  • No social media pixels — we don't track you via Facebook, Twitter, or similar.
  • No fingerprinting — we don't use device fingerprinting techniques.

4. How to Manage Cookies

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies.
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies.
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.
Note: Blocking essential cookies will prevent you from logging in.

5. Third-Party Cookies

  • Supabase — authentication and data storage.
  • Google Cloud — OAuth authentication (optional).
  • Resend — transactional email delivery.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology or legal requirements.

7. Contact

For questions about our use of cookies, contact us at privacy@youthtransformers.rw.

Data Processing Agreement

How we process personal data on behalf of your ministry.

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between your ministry ("Data Controller") and Youth Transformers ("Data Processor") for the use of the Service. It governs the processing of personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

1. Definitions

  • Personal Data — Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • Processing — Any operation performed on Personal Data (collection, storage, use, disclosure, deletion, etc.).
  • Data Subject — The individual whose Personal Data is being processed.
  • Sub-processor — A third party engaged by Youth Transformers to process Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.

2. Roles and Responsibilities

Your ministry is the Data Controller. You determine what Personal Data is stored in the Service, how it is used, and who has access. You are responsible for ensuring you have a lawful basis for processing.

Youth Transformers is the Data Processor. We process Personal Data only on your documented instructions, as necessary to provide the Service. We do not use your data for our own purposes.

3. Scope of Processing

  • Storing and organizing member, staff, and ministry records you provide.
  • Enabling features such as events, tasks, finances, and pastoral care management.
  • Authenticating users and managing access permissions.
  • Providing technical support and troubleshooting.
  • Complying with legal obligations applicable to us as a processor.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.
  • Row Level Security enforced at the database layer, ensuring logical data isolation per organization.
  • Role-based access control for all user actions.
  • Immutable audit logs for all sensitive operations.
  • Regular security reviews and updates.
  • Employee confidentiality obligations.

5. Sub-processors

We engage the following sub-processors to help deliver the Service:

  • Supabase — Database, authentication, and file storage (primary data store).
  • Vercel — Website hosting and content delivery.
  • Resend — Transactional email delivery.
  • Google Cloud — OAuth authentication (optional).

Each sub-processor is bound by contractual obligations that meet or exceed the protections in this DPA. We will notify you of any intended changes to our sub-processors so you have the opportunity to object.

6. Data Subject Rights

We will assist you in responding to Data Subject requests, including right of access (data export), right to rectification, right to erasure, right to restrict processing, and right to data portability. If you receive a request from a Data Subject, direct them to use in-app features where available, or contact us at dpo@youthtransformers.rw for support.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain Personal Data only for as long as your ministry account is active. Upon termination:

  • You have 30 days to export your data in a portable format (CSV / JSON).
  • After 30 days, all Personal Data is permanently deleted from active systems.
  • Backup copies are purged within 90 days.

8. International Transfers

Personal Data may be processed outside your jurisdiction by our sub-processors. We ensure appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and equivalent mechanisms, are in place for such transfers.

9. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a Personal Data breach, we will notify you without undue delay and no later than 72 hours after becoming aware of the breach; provide information about the nature of the breach and the data affected; describe measures taken or proposed; and cooperate with your notification obligations to supervisory authorities and Data Subjects.

10. Audit Rights

You have the right to audit our compliance with this DPA, subject to reasonable notice and confidentiality. For routine audits, we may provide our latest security documentation instead of on-site inspection.

11. Term and Termination

This DPA remains in effect for as long as we process Personal Data on your behalf. Upon termination, our obligations regarding data return and deletion survive.

12. Contact

For DPA-related questions, contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@youthtransformers.rw.
